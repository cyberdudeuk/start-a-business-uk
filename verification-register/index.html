        Method

        Every URL in the six content pages was extracted and requested. GOV.UK links were checked through the GOV.UK Content API; other links by HTTP request. Sources that refused automated requests were confirmed through search results retrieved the same day. Claims were chosen for their prominence: every headline statistic, every figure used in more than one page, and every rule founders act on. Each claim was matched automatically against the full text of its source, including PDF reports and spreadsheets, then read in context to confirm the meaning, not just the number. This pass did not re-check every sentence in the solution; statements not listed here rely on the original research sessions.

        Confirmed against source
        The claim was found, in context, in the source text retrieved during this check.
        Confirmed via search index
        The source blocked automated retrieval; the claim was confirmed in the source's text as returned by search on the same day.
        Corrected
        The check found an error or a better source, and the page was changed.
