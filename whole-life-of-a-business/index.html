Seven stages, one business, no single record

Government documents a business’s life one duty at a time. This page catalogues the duties, reliefs, routes and registers of every stage after starting: the recurring year, growth and its thresholds, selling and handing on, distress and recovery, winding down and liquidating, and the assets and liabilities that run through all of it. Each entry was retrieved on the day; the one that was not is recorded as a gap.
