              What this suggests for data and metadata

              This guide's own proposals, for discussion.

              1. Agree a business identifier strategyDecide whether to revive a single business identifier, or publish a crosswalk between company numbers, UTRs, VAT and PAYE references, as a first step towards what Australia, New Zealand and Singapore run.
              2. Open the name check across registersCombine Companies House and IPO name data behind one lookup, following New Zealand's ONECheck.
              3. Share licence data under local stewardshipAdopt a common licence data standard that local authorities maintain, as Canada's BizPaL partners do, rather than central re-collection.
              4. Publish formation statistics to an international standardReport online formations, duration, cost and abandonment in line with the measures EU states must report, so the UK can compare itself on evidence.

              Method and sources

              Comparators were chosen for strong benchmark scores (Singapore, Estonia), a named practice relevant to UK pain points (New Zealand, Australia, Canada, Denmark, Portugal), or a cautionary lesson (France). Ratings describe what each country offers founders, judged from official sources where they could be read; cells marked with an asterisk rely on reputable non-government guides. Ratings are relative and editorial, and cover only the countries shown.

              Leading
              A complete, working version of the capability that others cite or copy.
              Strong
              The capability exists and works for most founders, with limits.
              Partial
              Some of the capability exists, or it is split across services, or its launch was troubled.
              Weak or absent
              The capability is missing, or no published evidence of it was found.
              Not assessed
              Not checked on these pages; not a judgement.
