# Changelog

## 22 November 2026: two guides for people starting out, starting again, or needing to stay safe

- New guide, **Start and run a business safely**: for survivors of domestic abuse, stalking or harassment. What each
  way of setting up makes public, how to protect your details at Companies House (before you register, if you can),
  the data protection and electoral registers, training, exams and performing, devices and accounts, and money.
  Helplines for each nation, men and LGBT+ people, and the silent 999 call. Exit control (button, or Shift three
  times); no third-party request, no storage, no forms, no referrer.
- New guide, **Starting out, or starting again**: the enterprise route for 16 to 24 year olds (including those not
  in work, learning or training, and care leavers), support for young women and women, learning or retraining first
  in each nation, and coming back after caring, a sabbatical or illness, prison or the armed forces.
- Both are built from data (`data/guides.json`, 35 official or vetted sources, all link-checked on 22 November 2026)
  and link to the detail already in Your circumstances and Skills and communities instead of repeating it. The hub
  and Your circumstances signpost them.
- New checks: C9 (guides in step with their data), C10 (the safety guide's protections), C11 (every source official
  or vetted), C12 (signposts both ways). Each proved by a deliberate break. Accessibility (axe, WCAG 2.2 AA and best
  practice) clean in light and dark at 320, 390 and 1280 pixels.
