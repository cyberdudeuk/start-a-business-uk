      Method

      Each regulation source, guidance hub, sandbox, trade body and import or export requirement was checked by retrieving its own page and matching the wording recorded on the entry. Where a site blocks automated requests, that is recorded and the entry is marked as confirmed through search rather than against retrieved text. Trade bodies are a sample across sectors and nations, not the full population: there is no open register of UK trade associations, which is itself one of the gaps.

      Nothing here is legal advice. Rules on goods, licences and product marking change often, and the Trade Tariff, legislation.gov.uk and each regulator's own site are the live sources.

      Not an official government service. An independent, evidence-led guide by Emanuel Silva, part of Public Good Works. Every claim carries its source; check GOV.UK, nidirect, mygov.scot or gov.wales before you act.
