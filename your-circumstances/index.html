What this page does and does not do

The journey is the same for everyone: the same registrars, the same duties, the same deadlines. What differs is which parts bite hardest, what money is available, and what happens when the business meets the rest of a life. Everything here is traced to legislation or to GOV.UK. Where government does not have a view, this page says so rather than inventing one.
