Eight routes into owning a business, and how many are signposted

Every other part of this work assumes a founder starting something new. Most people arrive some other way: they buy a business, inherit one, buy a franchise, take over the one they work in, or walk out of a university lab with an invention. This catalogues all of them, plus the route out, with the front door for each where one exists and a plain statement where none does.
