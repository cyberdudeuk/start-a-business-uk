Navigate the graph

Two ways to move through the same data. Graph lays entities out by force and lets you follow relationships. The critical data assets, and where each one comes from

Every identifier a business collects on this journey, with the body that issues it, the moment it appears, what needs it next, and why losing it blocks the journey. Provenance is stated as the authoritative record, not the service that displays it.

Business glossary and thesaurus

Written for a founder rather than a lawyer, and shaped like a thesaurus: preferred term, alternative labels, broader and narrower terms, related terms. A term is here if a reader could reasonably be unsure, or if two parts of government use it differently.

Taxonomies

Seven controlled vocabularies, each with the policy for how its terms are applied. Where a national or international standard already exists, this uses it rather than inventing a parallel list.

The ontology

Ten classes and twelve properties, each aligned to a published vocabulary rather than invented. The alignments are proposals for review. No namespace has been minted and nothing here has been through a standards board.
