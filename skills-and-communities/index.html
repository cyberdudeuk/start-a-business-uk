Nobody asks for a qualification to register a business

Registration asks for identity, an address and a description of what you do. It never asks what you know. The qualification question arrives later, at the licence, the contract, the insurance and the loan. This catalogues how someone with no formal qualifications, education or training gets them — free routes, paid-while-you-learn routes, the tax rule that decides whether a self-employed person can afford to train — and the cohort communities that do the work no government service does.
