      Method and honesty about coverage

      Legal forms, activity triggers and requirements were checked one by one against GOV.UK, the Food Standards Agency, Food Standards Scotland and regulator pages, and each record states whether it is confirmed, partly confirmed or unverified. Support schemes come from the Department for Business and Trade's business finance and support finder, retrieved in full through the GOV.UK Search and Content APIs, with the finder's own industry, region, stage and size tags kept as published. Tax reliefs and allowances are not in that finder and were checked individually against HMRC guidance.

      This is a map of the landscape, not eligibility advice. Amounts, deadlines and conditions live on each scheme's own page and change often. Sector regulators outside the sample, devolved grant rounds and local authority schemes are not fully covered, and the gaps section says so.

      Not an official government service. An independent, evidence-led guide by Emanuel Silva, part of Public Good Works. Every claim carries its source; check GOV.UK, nidirect, mygov.scot or gov.wales before you act.
